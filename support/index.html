How to play

  • Pick a mode on the main menu: 1 Player plays against the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard); 2 Players share one screen, each child with their own controls; Cup is three matches in a row against the computer, for a trophy.
  • New to the game? The first match starts with a short practice on the court. How to play? on the main menu repeats it any time.
  • Pick an animal. Each one has a special skill: the kangaroo jumps super high, the cheetah is the fastest on the court, the elephant is hard to steal from…
  • Run: push the stick left or right with your thumb. It appears wherever your thumb touches. Left-handed? The joystick switch on the main menu swaps the stick and the button.
  • The button changes with the play and shows what it will do. With the ball: hold it, pull back to aim (a dotted line shows the shot) and let go. A quick tap shoots right away, and right under the hoop it's a dunk! Without the ball: tap next to the dribbler to steal, tap as the other animal goes up for a shot to block, or tap to jump.
  • The first to 5 baskets wins. Playing matches earns points: level up to unlock new animals, 12 in all, from a kangaroo to a dragon, and after that new ball designs.
  • The Album keeps every animal fact a match has shown. Can you find all 24?

For parents

  • Hoop Zoo has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never goes online. See the privacy policy.
  • The Parents area on the main menu is behind a parental gate (a multiplication question written out in words; type the answer on the number pad). It has the controls explained, the privacy policy, the open-source licences and a progress reset.
  • Reset progress: Parents → answer the question → tap Reset progress, then tap it again.
  • Language: the globe button on the main menu. The game opens in your device's language if it speaks it: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Indonesian, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified or Traditional Chinese.
  • No sound? Check the Sound button on the main menu, then make sure the device isn't in silent mode and the volume is up.
  • The game is played in landscape, on iPhone and iPad: turn the device sideways.
