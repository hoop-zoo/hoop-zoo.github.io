How to play

  • Pick a mode on the main menu: 1 Player plays against the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard); 2 Players share one phone, each child with their own controls.
  • Pick an animal. Each one has a special skill: the kangaroo jumps super high, the cheetah is the fastest on the court, the elephant is hard to steal from…
  • Run: push the stick left or right with your left thumb. It appears wherever your thumb touches.
  • The button on the right changes with the play and shows what it will do. With the ball: hold it, pull back to aim (a dotted arc shows the shot) and let go. A quick tap shoots right away, and right under the hoop it's a dunk! Without the ball: tap next to the dribbler to steal, tap when the other player jumps to shoot to block, or tap to jump.
  • The first to 5 baskets wins. Playing matches earns points: level up to unlock new players, 12 in all, from a kangaroo to a dragon.

For parents

  • Hoop Zoo has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never goes online. See the privacy policy.
  • The Parents area on the main menu is behind a parental gate (a multiplication question written out in words). It has the controls explained, the privacy policy and a progress reset.
  • Reset progress: Parents → answer the question → tap Reset progress twice.
  • No sound? Check the Sound button on the main menu, then make sure the iPhone isn't in silent mode and the volume is up.
  • The game is played in landscape: turn the phone sideways.
